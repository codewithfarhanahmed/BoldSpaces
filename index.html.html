How It Works

Your Vision, Our Craft

A seamless four-step journey from concept to the perfect finished piece in your home.

01
Consultation
We begin with a one-on-one design consultation — in person or virtual — to understand your space, taste, and requirements.
02
Design & Quote
Our team creates detailed 3D renders and a transparent itemised quote. You approve before a single nail is hammered.
03
Craftsmanship
Skilled artisans handcraft your piece using premium materials. You receive progress updates throughout the build.
04
Delivery & Setup
White-glove delivery, professional installation, and a final walkthrough to ensure every detail is perfect.
